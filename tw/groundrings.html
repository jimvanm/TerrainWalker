Ground rings

Detail in rings round you, as terrain engines do it: Canada's survey at 1, 2, 4 and 8 m close by, today's ground at 16 and 64 m beyond. A ring is fetched only while its detail can show on your screen, which depends on your height and the allowed error in pixels. Fly down from high up and back, and watch which rings come and go, what each costs, and whether you can see where they meet. T tints each ring its own colour.

Drag to look. W A S D or arrows to move, E or Space up, Q or C down, Shift for 5× faster, wheel for speed. G walks on the ground or flies. R goes back to the start.
