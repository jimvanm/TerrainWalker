Terrain data check

For each place: the height data at every detail level, zoom 10 to 15, against published facts. The terrain service mixes sources of very different quality (10 m in the US, 5 m in the Arctic, 30 m radar elsewhere); each place says which it uses. Spacings in green are no finer than the source, so they carry real measurements; past that, finer levels are only enlarged copies. Pictures are the same area at each level, lit from the north-west. Heights are as the app uses them, after "despike" removes stray points; "low before despike" shows what it removed. Downloads about 25 MB the first time, from the same free service the app uses.
